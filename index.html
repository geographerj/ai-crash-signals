About this page

This page tracks two kinds of evidence for a break in the AI-driven equity rally. Macro indicators come from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) and refresh automatically every weekday morning. AI-specific indicators, such as credit spreads on AI borrowers, memory prices and funding events, are entered by hand with an as-of date and marked stale after 45 days.

What the levels mean

Levels describe how many independent indicators agree. They say nothing about timing.

    Methodology and sources

    Lead times and false-alarm records are the reason no single indicator drives the level.

    S&P 500 vs 10-month average
    A lagging price rule. In our backtest since 1950 it held the 2000-02 and 2007-09 losses under 4%, but 52 of 56 exits were false alarms. FRED series SP500.
    Yield curve (10y minus 3m)
    The 2s10s curve inverted about six weeks before the March 2000 peak. The 10y-3m curve inverted about 15 months before October 2007 and had re-steepened by the peak. False alarms in 2019 and 2022-24 (780 days inverted, no recession). Nothing signaled in 1987. FRED series T10Y3M and T10Y2Y.
    Baa spread
    Widened about 58 bp in the two months before the 2000 peak and began widening about three months before 2007. False alarms in 2011, 2014-16, 2018 and 2025. FRED series BAA10Y.
    High-yield spread
    Tight at both peaks. Spikes to roughly 8-9% in 2011 and 2016 with no recession (secondary). FRED carries only three years of history for free. FRED series BAMLH0A0HYM2.
    Initial jobless claims
    Lagging. The 4-week average crossed 20% above its 52-week low about eight months after the 2000 peak and about six months after 2007. False alarms from hurricanes (2005, 2017) and 2023. FRED series ICSA.
    Sahm rule
    Lagging. It fired 15 months after 2000 and six months after 2007, with a false trigger in 2024. FRED series SAHMREALTIME.
    Fed path
    Every peak since 1987 came during tightening or just after the turn to easing. Shown as regime context, not a timer. FRED series DFF, DFEDTARL and DFEDTARU.
    AI credit, lab funding, GPU prices, lease cancellations
    No long history. The triggers are judgment calls modeled on the 2000-01 telecom debt repricing, the IPO window closing in 2000 and the 2001 bandwidth price collapse.
    DRAM prices
    Memory equities led contract prices by three to nine months in 2018 and 2022.
    TSMC revenue
    Coincident at best. It stayed strong after the January 2022 equity peak.
    Decline pacing
    In 2000 and 2007 only 13-23% of the S&P decline had happened one month after the peak. In 1987 and 2020, 87-99% happened within weeks.
    Sources
    FRED (series IDs above); FactSet Earnings Insight; TrendForce; SemiAnalysis; FINRA TRACE; investor.tsmc.com; company earnings releases.
